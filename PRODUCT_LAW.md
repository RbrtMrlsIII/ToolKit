# Product Law — Universal AGENT ToolKit

Product Law is the reconstruction of user-approved intent for Universal AGENT Toolkit. The user is the source of truth for product vision; policies, architecture, skills, evidence, and implementation remain subordinate to that intent.

## Authority

```text
USER COMMAND / VISION
        ↓
consult applicable Agents
        ↓
user approval for material direction
        ↓
PRODUCT_LAW.md
        ↓
MASTERPLAN.md checklist / execution slice
        ↓
skills + PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md
        ↓
O-R-U-C-A-V-E-A
        ↓
action / reconstruction
        ↓
validation
        ↓
evidence
        ↓
complete log / checkpoint / handover
        ↓
endorsement
        ↓
PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md growth
```

Agents may analyze, reconcile, and recommend before approval. They must not silently replace the user's intent with their own interpretation.

## Product meaning

Universal AGENT Toolkit is reusable operating infrastructure for WebAi seats and consuming projects. It supplies reusable skills, governance, continuity, knowledge discipline, adaptation, and responsibility allocation. It does not become the product authority for a consuming project.

## Scope boundary

Toolkit owns universal mechanisms only. Consuming projects retain authority over their own Product Law, domain rules, provider choices, credentials, schemas, implementation details, authorization, and user-facing semantics.

## Knowledge growth

`PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md` is the conceptual map of reusable relationships among fields, responsibilities, skills, artifacts/interfaces, providers/runtimes, verification, and consumer patterns.

Validated baselines and frozen execution slices trigger skill-coverage review. A reusable procedure may become a candidate skill or governance pattern only after validation and explicit endorsement. Project-specific findings remain downstream unless they are genuinely generalizable.

## Skill authority boundary

Skills instruct HOW work is performed. Policy, workspace rules, permissions, and project authority determine WHETHER action is allowed. Product Law determines WHAT IS TRUE.

A skill never grants authorization.

## Preservation

Historical evidence remains historical. Current canonical state may evolve through authorized changes. Candidate knowledge is not treated as endorsed knowledge. ToolKit evolution must preserve user authority, canonical structure, evidence traceability, and downstream autonomy.
